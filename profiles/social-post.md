# Profile: social post

No built-in baseline. Use the person's own past posts: `H baseline posts.txt -o voice.json`.

## What human posts look like

One observation, event or opinion, in the person's speaking voice, often incomplete. Specific: the place, the product, the number, the person. Platform habits vary and the writer's history shows them.

## How model-written posts differ

A hook line built to stop scrolling, then single-sentence paragraphs, a lesson drawn from a small event, a contrast frame ("it's not about X, it's about Y"), a list of three, a question to the audience at the end, emoji as bullets, and a row of hashtags.

## Rewriting

1. Ask what happened or what the person thinks. Write that, in the order they would say it aloud.
2. Remove the hook, the lesson and the closing question unless the person really wants replies to a question.
3. Join the one-line paragraphs back into normal ones.
4. Keep the specific detail and cut the generalisation drawn from it.
5. Use the emoji, tags and line breaks the person's own posts use, and no others.
